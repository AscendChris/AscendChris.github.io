I take on a small number of independent advisory engagements with organisations and startups outside financial services.

My work is focused on four areas:

🧭 Technology Risk & Control Effectiveness
For organisations and startups that are scaling quickly, inheriting complexity, or no longer trust that their documented controls reflect reality.

I help identify material technology risks, assess control design and effectiveness, expose missing or ineffective controls, and prioritise practical improvements.

For startups in particular, this can mean helping leadership understand where risk and control capability needs to mature as the business grows — without adding unnecessary bureaucracy.

⚙️ Major Technology Change & Implementation Risk
For organisations planning or delivering significant technology change.
I provide independent challenge on implementation risk, emerging failure modes, governance, control effectiveness through change, and incident readiness.

🤖 AI, Agents & Continuous Risk Monitoring
I advise on how AI and agentic systems can be used to make risk management more continuous and scalable — including continuous monitoring controls, improved risk detection, and reducing manual risk processes.

🪖 Specialist Risk Workshops
I also offer specialist risk workshops across a range of themes, combining practical technology risk experience with lessons drawn from eight years as a Royal Marine Commando.
These can be tailored around areas such as decision-making under pressure, resilience, challenge, risk identification, incident learning and operating effectively in complex environments.

🧠 How I Work
Engagements are small, time-boxed and advisory only.
I do not act as an outsourced risk function, own implementation, or access client production systems. The aim is to provide clear independent challenge, practical recommendations and better decision-making.

🔍 Independence & Conflict Checks
All work is undertaken independently and outside my full-time role at Monzo Bank.
Prospective clients complete a short due-diligence and conflict-of-interest questionnaire before any engagement begins.

I do not work with financial services, fintech, payments or banking organisations, and I will decline any engagement where a conflict cannot be appropriately managed.
