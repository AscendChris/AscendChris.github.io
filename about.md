Hello, I’m Chris 👋

I’m currently a Senior Technology Risk & Control Manager at Monzo Bank, covering Platform, Data and Security.

I specialise in technology risk, control design and effectiveness, governance, and major technology change/implementation — particularly where organisations are scaling quickly, complexity is increasing, or the documented risk picture doesn’t quite match reality.

More recently, I’ve become particularly interested in how AI and agentic systems can be used to scale risk management — especially through continuous monitoring controls, better risk detection, and more dynamic control frameworks.

I worked across risk and controls at Tier 1 banks including JP Morgan and Morgan Stanley.

Before Monzo, I worked across risk and controls at Tier 1 banks including JP Morgan and Morgan Stanley. Previous to that I spent eight years as a Royal Marine Commando and also trained as a Dietitian. 

Outside of work: aspiring vertical farmer 👨‍🌾🥕, obsessed with weighted pull-up records, and usually plotting some kind of extreme expedition 🏔️

💡 I take on a small number of independent advisory engagements outside of financial services, focused on technology risk, controls, major technology change and practical risk framework improvement.
